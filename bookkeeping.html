Questions

Before you sign up

Are you a CPA?

No. We're bookkeepers. We keep your books clean all year and hand your CPA tax-ready reports. Tax filing stays with your CPA or tax preparer.

What software do you use?

QuickBooks Online, Xero or Wave, whichever you already use. If you don't have one yet, we'll set one up with you.

How do you get access to my accounts?

Through read-only access you grant in your bookkeeping software, or statements you send us. We never need your online banking password.

What if I'm months behind?

That's what Catch-Up Cleanup is for. We review your accounts, quote a flat price, and bring every month current before monthly bookkeeping starts.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans are month to month with no long-term contract.
